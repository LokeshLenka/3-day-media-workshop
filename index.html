What you'll learn

By the end of the third session you'll have touched all three industry tools and built a small set of finished edits.

  • Clean up and retouch photos with layers, masks and non-destructive edits in Photoshop
  • Turn raw clips into a paced 30-second reel with captions, cuts and sound in CapCut
  • Turn ordinary footage into something that looks like cinema — balance, grade and finish in DaVinci Resolve
  • Choose the right tool for the job instead of forcing one app to do everything
  • Set export settings that stay sharp on Instagram, YouTube and in print
  • Build a repeatable editing workflow you can finish inside one hour

Daily plan

Each day stands alone — join one or join all three. Times are the same every day: 3:30 to 4:30 PM (1 hr). Every session needs its own app, so install it before you come — the steps are inside each day.
