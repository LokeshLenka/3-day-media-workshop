What you'll learn

By the end of the third session you'll have touched all three industry tools and worked through a small set of practice edits.

  • Clean up and retouch photos with layers, masks and non-destructive edits in Photoshop
  • Read a shot, choose the cut and set the pace — the craft behind every CapCut edit
  • Turn ordinary footage into something that looks like cinema — balance, grade and finish in DaVinci Resolve
  • Choose the right tool for the job instead of forcing one app to do everything
  • Set export settings that stay sharp on Instagram, YouTube and in print
  • Build a repeatable editing workflow you can finish inside one hour

08THU CapCut — beyond the cut: the art of editingThursday, October 8 · 3:30-4:30 PM · 1 hr
Prerequisites — tick them off before Thursday 0 / 7
Run of show — what we cover 60 MIN · 3:30 → 4:30 PM

This is not a CapCut tools tutorial: the craft of editing first, the software second. No prior experience needed and the raw footage is provided — here's how the hour is spent and how long each block takes.

  1. 5 min Introduction — what editing is, and why it matters before any tool
  2. 7 min Understanding the shot — wide, medium and close-up, B-roll, and how shot selection shapes meaning
  3. 10 min The art of the cut — when and why to cut — continuity, jump cuts, cutting on action, match cuts
  4. 8 min Pacing & rhythm — fast vs slow, beat-based cuts, anticipation, energy and pauses
  5. 7 min Sound & emotion — music, sound effects, ambience, dialogue and silence
  6. 5 min Visual mood — exposure, contrast and consistency — correction for the story, not filters
  7. 13 min Live CapCut edit — timeline, split/trim, speed, keyframes, text, captions, transitions, export
  8. 5 min Wrap-up — the key principles and where to practise them next

This hour is a live demonstration of Resolve's basic tools, page by page. Each block is shown hands-on and timed — here's the route through the software and how long it takes.
